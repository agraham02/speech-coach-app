# Tono Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Tono is a speaking-practice app made by Ahmad Graham ("I", "me"). This policy explains what Tono does with your information. I wrote it to be read, so it is plain on purpose.

## The short version

- **Your recordings stay on your phone.** Your videos, audio, transcripts, scores, face and voice measurements, and settings are stored on your device. I do not keep copies on a server.
- **Everything your phone measures is free and needs no account.** Nothing leaves your phone unless you turn on Tono AI.
- **Tono AI is optional.** When you let it judge a take, the words of that take (your phone's transcript, with its timing) and your phone's measurements of it are sent, as text, through my service to Google's Gemini. Your video and audio stay on your phone.
- **You never have to sign in.** When Tono AI or a purchase first needs one, Tono makes an anonymous account behind the scenes: a random ID with no name, email or password. Signing in with Apple or Google is optional, and only links that account so Tono Plus can follow you to another phone.
- **Tono AI is for people 18 and over.** If you tell Tono you are under 18, nothing is sent to Tono AI.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and I do not sell your information.**
- **If you buy Tono Plus or start its free trial,** Apple takes the payment and I never see your card details.
- **You can delete everything:** your account from Settings → Account & Plus, and the data on your phone from Settings or by deleting the app.

## Who to contact

Questions, or anything you want to know about this policy: **ahmadgrahamdev@gmail.com**

## What stays on your phone

Tono stores the following on your device only:

- **Recordings:** the videos you record, and the audio in them.
- **Transcripts and measurements:** the text of what you said, how fast you spoke, your pauses and filler words, your pitch and loudness, and scores built from them.
- **Face measurements:** while you record, Tono uses your camera to measure things like where you are looking, head position, and expression. This is calculated on your phone, saved as numbers, and used only to give you feedback. Tono does not identify or recognize you, and does not build a face profile.
- **What you tell Tono:** your name, your age range, your goals, what gets in your way, any event you are preparing for, how comfortable you feel speaking, and anything you write in the app (for example in Talk back, Rewind, or your "why").
- **Your progress:** streaks, practice history, and reminders you set.
- **Your Tono account's sign-in,** once there is one. It is kept in encrypted storage on your phone, and the key to it is in the iPhone Keychain.

Tono asks for permission to use the **camera**, **microphone** and **speech recognition**. You can change these at any time in iOS Settings. The app needs the camera and microphone to record, and works best with all three.

### On-device processing

Turning your speech into text and analyzing your voice and face happens on your phone, using a speech model that you download once. No audio or video is uploaded for this. The tips Tono gives you without Tono AI are worked out on your phone from those measurements.

## What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

### 1. Tono AI (optional)

Tono AI judges a take the way a coach would: whether your answer had a shape, stayed on the point and landed, and what to change next. Tono asks your permission once, before anything is sent, and shows exactly what goes. You can turn it off in Settings → Tono AI, and turn it off for any single take on its save screen.

**What is sent for a judged take:**

- the words of the take: your phone's transcript, with the timing of each word (so pauses can be judged);
- your phone's measurements of it: pace, pauses, filler counts, pitch, loudness, eye contact, expression and head movement, as numbers;
- the prompt or script you were answering, what the prompt asked you to do, and any plan or notes you wrote while preparing;
- unless you switch it off in Settings, a short note about you so the feedback fits: where you want to speak better, what gets in your way, any upcoming event (including any note you wrote about it), your training and comfort level, whether English is your first language, and any note you wrote about your voice. It does not include your name, your age, your "why", or your Talk back entries;
- if you told Tono you stutter, a note so your speech is judged accordingly.

**Your video and audio are not sent.** One exception, which you would have to choose: a developer setting in the app can judge from the video instead. Then the video (with its audio) goes from your phone straight to Google, together with the same transcript and measurements, and is deleted from Google as soon as the take is judged (if judging fails, Google removes it within 48 hours at the latest). A take with no transcript can only be judged that way.

**Exercises.** When you ask for notes on a Rewind or a Say it short drill, or have Tono AI check a Word Chain or Bridge Chain, or mark a text in Mark it up, the words involved (and the prompt or passage) are sent the same way, as text. Never audio or video.

**Live conversation (Tono Plus).** In a live conversation, your phone listens and turns what you say into words; your voice itself is not sent. The words of the conversation so far (yours and your partner's), the scene and the move you are practicing go the same way, as text, so your partner can answer, and the partner's reply comes back as text and as a voice made by Google's Gemini text-to-speech. At the end, the same words go once more for a short note on the moves you made. Nothing of the conversation is kept on my service; the app keeps the process numbers on your phone (how soon you replied, whether you asked), not the words.

**How it travels.** Everything goes through my service, which writes the instructions for the AI, sends them with your words and numbers to Google's Gemini, and passes Google's reply back to your phone. My service does not save your words, your numbers or the reply. It keeps only a record that a call happened (see below).

**Google.** I use Google's paid Gemini service. Under Google's terms for paid use, Google does not use what you send, or its replies, to improve its products. Google logs prompts and responses for a limited period, only to detect and prevent abuse of its service and for legal requirements; its terms do not give an exact length of time. See Google's terms for the current rules.

**The model can change.** I choose the Gemini model on my server, and may change it, or the instructions, without an app update. Each judged take records which model and which version of the instructions judged it.

### 2. Your Tono account

There is no account until Tono AI or a purchase needs one. Then Tono makes an **anonymous account**: a random user ID, with no name, email or password. You never see a sign-up screen. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google (Settings → Account & Plus), that sign-in is linked to the same account, so Tono Plus can follow you to a new phone.

What I hold about an account:

- **A user ID,** and, only if you signed in with Apple or Google, which one you used, the email address from it (Apple may give me a private relay address instead of your real one), and the name or picture that account shares, if any. Apple and Google run the sign-in itself and tell me only that it was you.
- **Your plan:** whether you have Tono Plus or its free trial, which plan, when the current period started and ends, and whether it was a test purchase.
- **A record of each Tono AI call:** when it happened, what kind it was (a judged take, an exercise, a mark-up, a turn of a live conversation), which model ran, how many tokens it used and what that cost, and whether it worked. For a live conversation it also holds a random conversation ID made on your phone and the seconds counted for that turn, which is how the conversation minutes work. It holds none of your video, words or results. These records are how the monthly allowances and the cost limit work.
- **A short-lived upload record,** only if a take is judged from its video, linking that video to your account while it is judged. It is removed when judging finishes, or when you delete your account.
- **A Sign in with Apple token,** only if you signed in with Apple, stored encrypted and used only to cancel Apple's link to Tono when you delete your account.

Your age range is never sent: Tono uses it on your phone to decide whether Tono AI is offered.

My service is hosted by Supabase in the United States. Like any online service, Supabase and Google can see the connection details of a request, such as your IP address and the time. My service's own logs record errors and not your content.

I use this only to run Tono AI and Tono Plus: to know which account a call is for, to apply the allowances, to keep the cost in check, and to find and fix problems. I do not use it for advertising or tracking.

### 3. Tono Plus and the free trial

- **Apple takes the payment.** You buy, or start the 14-day free trial, through the App Store with your Apple ID. I never see your card or other payment details. Apple decides who can have the free trial (one per Apple ID).
- **RevenueCat manages the subscription.** I use RevenueCat, a subscription service, to check purchases with Apple. It receives your Tono user ID (a random ID, not your name or email), what you bought, whether it is a trial, the App Store receipt, and your device type and iOS version. It receives none of your recordings, words or scores. RevenueCat works on my behalf and stores this data in the United States.
- **No sign-in is needed to buy.** The purchase belongs to your Tono account, anonymous or linked. To keep Plus on another phone, sign in with Apple or Google, or use Restore purchases.
- **Restoring and cancelling.** "Restore purchases" asks Apple and RevenueCat to find your past purchases. You cancel, including during the free trial, in your Apple ID's subscription settings. I cannot cancel it for you, and deleting your Tono account does not cancel it.
- **Test builds.** In TestFlight builds, purchases are Apple "sandbox" purchases and do not charge you.

### 4. Checking what's on offer

When the app starts without an account, it asks my service whether Tono Plus is on sale and which features are on. That request carries nothing about you (the service sees only the connection, such as your IP address) and nothing is stored.

### 5. The speech model download

The first time you use Tono, it downloads a speech-recognition model from Hugging Face. Hugging Face can see ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, as with any download. No recording or personal data is sent.

### 6. Apple's speech recognition

Some live drills use iOS speech recognition. Where your iPhone supports it, this happens on the device. If it does not, Apple may process the audio on its servers under Apple's privacy policy.

### 7. Apple services

If you install Tono through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple handles the download, and may share crash reports or tester feedback with me if you have allowed that in iOS. Tono's reminders are scheduled on your phone; they are not push notifications from a server.

## What I do not do

- I do not store your recordings, transcripts, scores or answers on a server.
- I do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app.
- I do not sell or share your information.
- I do not see or store your payment details.
- I do not ask you to create an account or sign in.

## Keeping it and deleting it

- **Deleting a take:** when you delete a recording (a take in the Library, or a Rewind), everything about it is erased from your phone for good: the video and audio, the transcript, the word timings, the scores, the coach's and Tono AI's notes, and the entries in your progress history that came from it. There is no 30-day hold and no hidden copy. You get about 8 seconds to tap Undo; the erase is finished when that passes, when you leave the app, or the next time it opens. The one thing that stays is the date: so a deleted take does not break your streak, the app keeps a bare calendar date for that day, with nothing else attached. Skill levels, stages and your path that were already worked out are not recalculated, but the deleted take counts toward nothing from then on. If the take's video was at Google to be judged, Tono asks Google to delete it at the same time (Google removes it within 48 hours at the latest in any case). My service's record of an AI call holds only counts and cost, never your words or video, so it has nothing of the take in it to delete. Any copy you shared yourself, such as an exported file you sent somewhere, is outside the app and stays where you put it.
- **Freeing up space:** a separate choice that deletes only a take's video and its audio and keeps its transcript and scores. Settings can do this automatically for old videos after the number of days you choose (two "keepsake" takes, the best and the toughest of each month, keep their video).
- **Delete everything on your phone:** Settings → Your data → Reset app erases all recordings, transcripts, scores, progress and settings, and asks Google to delete any video still held for judging. Deleting the app removes all of it, including the speech model.
- **Export:** Settings → Your data lets you save a copy of your practice data.
- **Your account:** Settings → Account & Plus → Delete account removes your account, your plan record and the record of your Tono AI calls, removes your RevenueCat record, and cancels Apple's link to Tono if you signed in with Apple. It does not cancel a subscription: do that in your Apple ID's subscription settings. Your recordings and history on the phone are not touched. If you cannot open the app, email me and I will delete it.
- **Nothing is kept after you delete.** I keep no copy of a deleted account.
- **At Google:** anything sent to be judged is under Google's retention rules for its paid service, described above.

## Your choices and rights

You can use all of Tono's practice, measurements and tips with no account. If you have an account, you can ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it, and deleting it yourself in the app is the quickest way. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over your information, such as the right to complain to a data-protection authority. Write to the address above and I will answer.

## Security

Everything sent to Tono AI travels over encrypted connections. Your data lives in the app's private storage on your phone, protected by your device's passcode and iOS. The key that lets my service use Google's Gemini lives only on my server and never on your phone. My service refuses requests that do not come from a Tono account, and my database is locked so that nothing but my service can read it. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your phone locked.

If your device is backed up through iCloud or to a computer, your practice data may be included in that backup, under Apple's terms. The speech model is excluded from backups.

## Children and teenagers

Tono asks your age range when you set it up. Google's terms for the Gemini service that Tono AI uses require users to be 18 or older, so if you say you are under 18, Tono AI is not offered and nothing you record is sent anywhere: everything runs on your phone. Tono is not meant for children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has used Tono AI, write to me and I will delete the account.

## International use

My service and RevenueCat run in the United States, and Google may process data in other countries. That is governed by their policies. If you are elsewhere, using Tono AI means your account record and the words sent to be judged are handled there.

## Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will update the date at the top. If a change affects what is sent off your phone, Tono will ask for your permission again.
